Privacy Policy — The Hive

Last updated: September 22, 2026

The Hive ("the game", "we", "us") is a mobile game published by The Hive, Inc. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used. The Hive is not directed at children and is rated for players aged 13 and up.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. On first launch, the game creates an anonymous account (via Firebase Authentication) so your progress can be saved to the cloud. No name, email or phone number is required to play.
  • Game save data. Your in-game progress (currencies, upgrades, settings) is stored in the cloud (Firebase/Firestore) and tied to your anonymous account ID, so progress can be restored on reinstall.
  • Advertising data. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and device information to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase data. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive a purchase token to verify and grant your purchase; we do not receive your payment card details.
  • Analytics. We use Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay patterns (e.g. which tutorial step players reach, session length) so we can improve the game. This data is aggregated and not used to identify you personally.
  • Crash reports. We use Sentry to collect crash and error reports so we can fix bugs. Reports may include device model, OS version, and the state of the game at the time of the crash.

How we use this information

We use the information above only to: operate and save your game progress, process purchases, show and measure rewarded ads, fix bugs, and improve game balance and pacing. We do not sell your personal information.

Data sharing

We share data only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, Sentry), each acting under their own privacy policies, and only as needed to provide the functions described here.

Data retention and deletion

Save data is retained while your account is active. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below with your in-game account ID (found in Settings) and we will remove your cloud save.

Children's privacy

The Hive is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.
